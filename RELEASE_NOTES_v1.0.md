# SARTHI OS v1.0.0 — Release Candidate Notes

## Executive Overview
**SARTHI OS** is a high-performance, full-stack Personal Life & Business Operating System. It unifies Executive Planning, Smart Habit Tracking, Strategic Goals Management, Daily Reflective Journaling, Interactive Notes, Voice AI Coaching, and Calendar Synchronization into a cohesive, responsive experience.

---

## Key Feature Highlights
1. **Executive Home Dashboard**: Daily streak counter, priority tasks overview, quick action hub, and real-time productivity score.
2. **Planner Module**: Daily time-blocked schedule, priority filters, task category chips, overdue auto-detection, and quick capture.
3. **Smart Habit Tracker**: Daily completion tracking, current & longest streak metrics, completion heatmaps, and category filters.
4. **Strategic Goals**: Clean quarterly goal cards (`Q1`-`Q4`), progress bar with animated completion fill, milestone checklist, and user-friendly due dates (`📅 Due: 30 Sep 2026`).
5. **Reflective Journal**: Daily mood tracking, reflective prompts, category tags, and search indexing.
6. **Integrated Notes**: Markdown editor, tag categorization, duplicate/pin capabilities, and search filtering.
7. **Smart Calendar**: Separate date navigation module with Month, Week, Day, and Agenda views. Multi-colored task/meeting/habit count badges (`🔵2 🟢1 🟠3`) and grouped timeline by time of day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
8. **Voice AI Coach**: Voice recognition, multi-lingual support (English, Hindi, Gujarati), clean voice responses without robotic symbols or markdown noise.
9. **Offline First PWA & Android TWA**: Offline caching service worker, manifest `com.sarthi.app`, digital asset links ready.
